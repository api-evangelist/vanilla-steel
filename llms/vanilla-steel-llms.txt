# Vanilla Steel

> Vanilla Steel is a Berlin-based B2B steel trading platform (Vanilla Steel GmbH, seedcamp-backed) that matches European steel buyers and sellers, arranges financing, and manages logistics end-to-end. It connects 500+ European suppliers with 5,000+ buyers and sellers, promising a best offer within 48 hours from a single, format-agnostic request. Its SaaS products — the RFQ Classifier (Outlook add-in), RFQ Extractor (OAuth mailbox ingestion for Microsoft 365 and Gmail), and Quoting App — use AI to turn inbound Requests for Quotation into structured trading workflows. Vanilla Steel publishes no public developer API.
